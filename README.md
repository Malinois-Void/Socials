## 🐾 About Me

Hey, I’m Void. A blend of Belgian Malinois and Alligator — mostly fur, with just a hint of scales. 

Trained with a military K9 background, I've got the focus, loyalty, and protective instincts of a working dog—just paired with an alligator's love for stealth and lounging.

I tend to stay in the shadows, being pretty quiet and observant at first. It takes a minute to charge my social battery, but once I warm up to people and get comfortable, that high-energy hybrid drive really kicks in. 

Happy to make new friends, so feel free to say hi! 👋
